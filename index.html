THE ARTIST

THE GHOST
HAS A VOICE.

BURDEN GHOST is the artistic project of Jonathan Perez, a Salvadoran artist born on October 3, 1993, in the small town of Nueva Guadalupe, El Salvador.

His music is built around isolation, struggle, transformation and the parts of ourselves we usually keep hidden. BURDEN GHOST turns those experiences into dark, emotionally driven songs that move between vulnerability and aggression.

The sound is for anyone who has felt the weight, faced the darkness and kept moving anyway.
