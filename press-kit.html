ABOUT BURDEN GHOST

THE STORY
BEHIND THE GHOST.

BURDEN GHOST is the artistic project of Jonathan Pérez, a Salvadoran artist born on October 3, 1993, in Nueva Guadalupe, El Salvador.

His music explores isolation, struggle, transformation and the parts of ourselves that are usually kept hidden. The project turns those experiences into dark, emotionally driven songs that move between vulnerability and aggression.

BURDEN GHOST is built around a simple idea: the weight we carry can become something we create with. The music speaks to listeners who have faced darkness and kept moving.
